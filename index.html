About

Faith & Family

Noah lives for Jesus Christ. His faith is the foundation of who he is and shapes how he treats people, approaches challenges, and lives his life. His priorities are simple: God, Family, School, and Baseball.

Family is a huge part of who Noah is. He is close with his sister Alyssa, his parents, Megan and Shane, and his grandparents. He enjoys being at home with family and friends. Noah is a very caring person, a loving son and brother, a loyal friend, and a good teammate and leader in the dugout.

School, Work & Character

School is extremely important to Noah. He takes his responsibilities as a student very seriously, carries a 3.96 GPA on a 4.0 scale, and plans to pursue a bachelor's degree in mechanical engineering.

Noah also finds time to work and earn his own money. He and his dad, Shane, work together in a local family-owned operation performing preventative maintenance and mechanical repairs on home standby generators. The work reflects his mechanical mindset, work ethic, and ability to figure things out and make things happen.

Baseball has been a huge part of Noah's life since he was five years old. A 2027 senior and four-year letterman at Klein Collins High School, Noah has competed at the highest level throughout most of his baseball career. While baseball is a huge part of his life, it does not define him. His faith, family, friendships, character, and work ethic are the foundation of the young man he has become.

On the baseball field, Noah stands 6'6", bats from the right side, and plays center field. He has a plus bat, shows power, and consistently produces hits for extra bases. He brings excellent speed as well, running a 6.54 60. Noah's speed and baserunning instincts make him a legitimate threat and give him the ability to change the outcome of games.

Noah started his high school baseball career as a catcher, where he developed an extremely strong baseball IQ, but due to his growth, has chosen to transition to center field. Noah sees the game differently, understands situations quickly, anticipates well, and puts himself in good positions to make an impact. His instincts show up in his defensive reads, baserunning, positioning, and in-game decision-making. His combination of size, speed, range, and instincts allows him to cover more ground and make tough plays in the gaps. Noah moves exceptionally well and has the ability to impact the game on both sides of the baseball.

Noah plays for the Houston Wildcatters during the summer, competing against some of the highest-level players from across the country.

Noah works hard to get better, whether he's on the field, in the weight room, or in the batting cage. Noah plays the game the right way all the time, which is all out.
